# Review and ship

These pages are for library staff and reviewers who start runs in the browser, check the AI's decisions and release the finished PDF. They also cover fixing a PDF by hand in Acrobat and bringing it back.

- [The home page](home.md): upload PDFs and start runs and batches in the browser.
- [The app](app.md): review a run and use its buttons in the browser.
- [Reviewing the work order](review-step.md): check the AI's decisions before they are applied.
- [Editing the PDF by hand](hand-edits.md): fix something in Acrobat and bring the PDF back.
- [Accessibility evaluation and release](release.md): what "ready to ship" means and how a PDF is released.
